How it runs

From scope to supply in five stages.

Each stage ends with a document you can put in the file — a spec, a feasibility report, a development report, a qualification package.

01
Scope
Target, cell type, scale and grade are fixed in a written spec.
02
Feasibility
Bench-scale proof of concept, usually 3–6 weeks.
03
Process development
Design of experiments, analytical method set.
04
Qualification
Pilot lot, stability, tech-transfer package.
05
Supply
GMP lot under quality agreement, with change control.
