01Models

Three ways to work with us.

Pick the one that matches how you already sell. We would rather have one committed partner than three competing ones in the same country.

01

Authorised distributor

You hold stock, sell into your territory, and provide first-line technical support. Territory is defined by country or region and reviewed annually.

  • Exclusive or non-exclusive by territory
  • Local stock and logistics
  • First-line application support
Fits: established reagent or instrument distributors with a life-science catalogue.
02

OEM & private label

Our reagents, our batch records, your brand. Formulation and packaging are fixed; labelling, documentation and market positioning are yours.

  • Your label, our manufacturing
  • Batch records and COA under your name
  • Minimum order applies
Fits: companies with a brand and a channel but no in-house manufacturing.
03

Referral & academic

You introduce a programme, we run the technical and commercial conversation, and the introduction is recognised for the life of the account.

  • No stock commitment
  • Project-based recognition
  • Technical team joins the first call
Fits: consultants, core facilities and academic groups.
02What you get

Support that is written down.

Channel agreements fail on the unwritten parts. These six are in the contract, not in the sales deck.

Territory

Defined by agreement, reviewed annually — not by whoever emails first.

Technical training

Application scientists train your team on the workflows, not just the catalogue.

Marketing assets

Datasheets, imagery, DMF summaries and protocol notes, cleared for your market.

Supply commitment

Stock reservation and lead-time commitments written into the agreement.

Regulatory support

Letters of authorisation and quality documentation for your registrations.

Co-branded collateral

Local-language material built with your team, not handed to you.

03Qualification

What we look for.

Not a checklist to tick — these are the four things that decide whether a partnership works six months in.

Existing customer base

An installed base in cell therapy, bioprocessing or diagnostics — not a cold start.

Technical capability

Someone on your team can answer a protocol question without escalating.

Regulatory familiarity

You have handled IVD or RUO registration in your territory before.

Logistics

Cold-chain storage and a track record of compliant delivery.
