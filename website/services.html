02Lab automation

Three tiers of capacity.

Start at bench scale and move up as the programme grows. Every tier runs under the same methods and the same quality system, so moving up does not mean re-qualifying the work.

Available now
Bench-scale, expert-led

A named scientist runs your design at the bench — small batches, tight iteration, judgement on every decision that the model cannot make.

  • Named application scientist per project
  • 1–5 L bench scale
  • 7–10 day typical turnaround
Rolling out 2026–27
Automated screening

Liquid handling and digital work instructions take over the repetitive transfer steps, so the same team carries more concurrent projects without losing oversight.

  • Automated liquid handling on core assays
  • Digital work instructions
  • Concurrency up ~3×

Network throughput

A scheduling layer routes batches across qualified sites. Throughput becomes a function of the network, not of one building.

  • Multi-site capacity routing
  • Standardised data return
  • Order-to-result SLA
What "automation" means here: liquid handling and scheduling across qualified sites. Scientists stay in the loop for design and exception handling — the model proposes, the platform executes, the scientist decides.
03Capacity network

One order. Many benches.

Academic core facilities and specialist laboratories already own capacity that sits idle between grants. We qualify that capacity, standardise the data it returns, and route work to it — so a customer sees one contract and one report.

Discuss a volume programme
01
Order intake

One specification, one contract, one point of contact — regardless of where the work runs.

02
Qualification

Each partner facility is audited on method fit, data format and turnaround before it receives work.

03
Routing

The scheduler assigns each batch to the bench with the right capability and free capacity.

04
Return

Results come back in one format, under our quality system, with our name on the report.

What a partner facility has to pass before it receives work
Method fit

The facility can run the assay to our written method, not an approximation of it.

Data format

Results return in our schema — raw where it matters, summarised where it does not.

Quality system

Documented training, equipment qualification and deviation handling.

Turnaround

A committed window per batch, with escalation when it slips.

Why we are building it this way: a reagent supplier that also controls capacity can commit to a delivery date. One that does not, cannot — however good its catalogue is.
