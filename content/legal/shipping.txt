Shipping and Returns

Dispatch times: In-stock items are usually dispatched within five working days of order confirmation, and always within fifteen working days of cleared payment. Items that are not held in stock are quoted with a lead time before you are asked to pay.

Shipping costs: Shipping is quoted separately and confirmed before dispatch. Most of our products ship on dry ice or with cold packs, so the cost depends on destination, weight and the coolant required. For card orders we confirm the shipping cost by email before sending the payment link.

Cold chain: Products marked for storage at 2–8 °C or −20 °C are shipped with the appropriate coolant. Please make sure someone can receive the delivery and place it into the correct storage immediately on arrival.

What can be returned: Unopened products in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery, subject to a 15% restocking charge unless the return is due to our error. Opened products, custom or made-to-order items, and temperature-controlled products cannot be returned, because their integrity cannot be guaranteed once they leave controlled storage.

Damaged or incorrect items: If a product arrives damaged, short, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 30 days of delivery and include photographs where possible. We will replace the item or refund it, at our option. Return shipping is at our cost where the error is ours.

Refunds: Card payments are refunded to the original card. Bank transfer payments are refunded to the account the payment came from. We process refunds within five working days of approval; your bank may take longer to show the credit.

Cancelling an order: Before dispatch you can cancel at no charge by replying to the order confirmation email. After dispatch, the return conditions above apply.
